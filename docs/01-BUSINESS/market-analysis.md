## 1. Executive Summary

The Scrum Ceremony Platform occupies a high-growth intersection between the collaborative whiteboard market ($3.17B → $9.59B, 2025–2031, ~20.3% CAGR) and the scrum/agile tooling market ($3.57B → $10.3B, 2024–2035, ~10.1% CAGR). The platform targets a persistent, underserved pain point: Scrum Masters need purpose-built ceremony orchestration — not generic whiteboards retrofitted with templates.

**Key Findings:**

| Finding | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| The retro/ceremony niche is structurally underserved by horizontal tools | Vertical specialization commands pricing power |
| 3 consistent market gaps exist across all competitors | Clear whitespace for a purpose-built platform |
| Per-team pricing is the dominant model; per-user is a differentiator | Pricing architecture is a strategic lever |
| Enterprise readiness (SOC 2, SAML, SCIM) is table stakes at $50K+ ACV | Compliance investment unlocks mid-market/enterprise |
| AI-powered facilitation is nascent across all competitors | First-mover advantage available in AI-augmented ceremonies |

**Bottom-Up SOM (Year 5, Base Case):** $8.2M ARR from ~340 paying teams across mid-market segment.

## 7. Strategic Gaps Analysis

### 7.1 Three Consistent Market Gaps

After scoring all 9 competitors across 7 dimensions, three gaps emerge consistently — no competitor scores above 3/5 on these combined capabilities:

---

#### **Gap 1: Closed-Loop Action Tracking → Jira/Linear Integration**

| Evidence | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| TeamRetro | 4/5 on action tracking but limited bidirectional sync |
| Miro | 2/5 — explicitly tells users to track actions elsewhere |
| MS Whiteboard | 1/5 — no action tracking whatsoever |
| Parabol | 3/5 — basic but no deep issue tracker integration |
| EasyRetro/GoRetro | 1–2/5 — no integration |

**The Gap:** No competitor offers **bidirectional, real-time sync** between retro action items and Jira/Linear/GitHub Issues, with automatic status reflection back into the ceremony workspace. Actions live in silos.

**Our Opportunity:** Build native, bidirectional integrations where action items created in retros automatically generate tickets, and ticket status changes retroactively update the ceremony board. This closes the "retro → action → follow-up" loop that is currently broken.

---

#### **Gap 2: Cross-Ceremony Analytics & Team Health Scoring**

| Evidence | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| TeamRetro | 5/5 on health checks but siloed to retros only |
| Parabol | 3/5 — basic analytics, no cross-ceremony view |
| Miro | 2/5 — no team health concept |
| Retrium | 3/5 — retro-specific analytics only |
| Neatro | 3/5 — limited historical tracking |

**The Gap:** No competitor provides a **unified team health score** that aggregates data across retros, standups, planning, and reviews into a single longitudinal view. Analytics are ceremony-specific, not team-specific.

**Our Opportunity:** Create a **Team Health Index (THI)** — a composite metric (sentiment trend, action completion rate, blocker recurrence, participation equity, velocity correlation) visible to Scrum Masters and engineering leadership. This becomes the executive dashboard that justifies the tool's ROI.

---

#### **Gap 3: AI-Augmented Facilitation (Not Just AI Summarization)**

| Evidence | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| TeamRetro | AI grouping of similar items (basic) |
| Parabol | AI summary generation |
| Miro | AI-assisted diagramming (horizontal) |
| Retrium | No AI features |
| Mentimeter | No AI features |

**The Gap:** Current "AI features" are limited to **post-hoc summarization** (grouping, summarizing). No competitor offers **real-time facilitation assistance** — AI that suggests facilitation techniques, detects participation imbalance, recommends retro formats based on team state, or prompts the Scrum Master during the ceremony.

**Our Opportunity:** Build an **AI Facilitation Co-Pilot** that operates in real-time:
- Suggests retro formats based on team health signals
- Detects low participation and nudges the facilitator
- Recommends time-box adjustments
- Surfaces recurring themes across ceremonies
- Generates facilitation playbooks for new Scrum Masters

| Moat Type | Strength | Build Timeline | Sustainability |
|-----------|----------|----------------|----------------|
| **Data Network Effects** | Medium-High | 18–24 months | High — team health data improves with history |
| **Workflow Lock-in** | High | 6–12 months | High — action tracking + integrations create switching costs |
| **Compliance Moat** | High | 6–9 months | Very High — SOC 2/SAML/SCIM takes 6+ months and $50K+ to replicate |
| **AI Facilitation Data Flywheel** | Very High | 12–18 months | Very High — more ceremonies → better AI → more ceremonies |
| **Vertical Specialization** | Medium | 3–6 months | Medium — replicable but takes time |
| **Integration Ecosystem** | Medium-High | 12–18 months | High — each integration increases stickiness |

### 9.2 Moat Build Sequence

```
Phase 1 (0–6 mo):    Vertical specialization + Workflow lock-in
                     └─ Purpose-built ceremonies, action tracking, basic integrations

Phase 2 (6–12 mo):   Compliance moat + Integration ecosystem
                     └─ SOC 2 Type 2, SAML SSO, Jira/Linear/GitHub deep integrations

Phase 3 (12–18 mo):  AI flywheel ignition
                     └─ AI Facilitation Co-Pilot trained on ceremony data

Phase 4 (18–24 mo):  Data network effects
                     └─ Team Health Index becomes industry benchmark
                     └─ Cross-org anonymized benchmarking (opt-in)

Phase 5 (24–36 mo):  Platform moat
                     └─ Marketplace for ceremony templates & facilitator community
                     └─ API ecosystem for third-party ceremony apps
```
